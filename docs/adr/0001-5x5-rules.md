# ADR 0001 — 5x5 English draughts rules

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-27
- **Deciders:** author, opencode
- **Related:** `docs/00-brief.md`, `docs/plan.md`

## Context

The first milestone needs a game small enough for tabular Q-learning and exact
tests, while still exercising captures, kings, promotion, and draws. Standard
8x8 checkers is far too large for a first table. The rules must be unambiguous
before move generation or evaluation code is written.

## Decision

Use a 5x5 English draughts variant.

### Board and starting position

- The board is 5x5. Only the 13 dark squares are playable.
- Coordinates are `(row, col)`, both 0-based. Row 0 is Black's back rank; row 4
  is White's back rank.
- Dark squares satisfy `(row + col) % 2 == 0`.
- Each side starts with 5 men:
  - Black: `(0,0), (0,2), (0,4), (1,1), (1,3)`.
  - White: `(4,0), (4,2), (4,4), (3,1), (3,3)`.
- The middle row starts empty.
- Black moves first. Black moves toward increasing rows; White moves toward
  decreasing rows.

### Movement and captures

- A man moves one square diagonally forward.
- A king moves one square diagonally in any direction.
- Captures are mandatory.
- A man captures forward only; a king captures in any direction.
- A multiple capture continues from the landing square until no capture remains.
- If more than one capture sequence exists, any legal sequence may be chosen.
  There is no maximum-capture requirement.
- Reaching the opponent's back rank promotes a man to king. Promotion ends the
  move, even during a multiple capture.

### Ending a game

- A side loses if it has no pieces or no legal moves.
- A game is drawn after threefold repetition of the same position with the same
  side to move, or after 80 plies without a capture or promotion. A ply is one
  player's move.
- Draws are reported, not treated as failures.

## Consequences

- The board is small enough to inspect, test, and use with a sparse Q-table, and
  still contains the rules that matter for learning.
- This is not standard 8x8 checkers. Results and openings do not transfer
  directly to the full game.
- The starting position, promotion timing, and draw conditions must be encoded
  in known-position and rules tests.
- The variant may be imbalanced. Evaluation must use both colours and a fixed
  opening suite.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Full 8x8 checkers:** tabular Q-learning cannot scale to it; deferred.
- **6x6 variant:** closer to 8x8 structure, but a much larger state space for
  the first table.
- **8x8 endgames:** standard rules, but a more complex position generator and
  curriculum.
- **No draw rule:** games can loop forever and poison training and evaluation.
